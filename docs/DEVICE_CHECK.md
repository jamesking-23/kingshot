# Device check (the only thing I need from you per build)

About 3 minutes, once per milestone, not per phase.

1. Install the latest `KingShot-debug-apk` from the Actions page. Open the app and allow the camera.
2. Tap **Lab**, then **Run self-check**. Keep the phone still, pointed at a normal scene, for about 30 seconds. It takes the test photos by itself and grades them from the receipts.
3. Tap **Export bundle** and share the zip to me. Nothing else is needed.

Optional, 1 minute, only if you have time (these cannot be graded by the app because they need eyes):
- Do the top buttons respond, and does the picture look upright?
- Does tapping the picture show a ring and refocus?
- In a dim room, does the flash fire when set to On?

What the self-check can and cannot tell me
- **Can:** photos saved, JPEG and DNG pairs, whether exposure and zoom requests were applied, which lens was active, whether processing requests came back honored, save times, crashes (the log).
- **Cannot:** picture quality, whether a DNG opens in an independent RAW tool, flash, focus accuracy, level direction, interruptions such as phone calls. Those stay marked **unverified** until you look, and I will not claim them.

This supersedes DV-101..109 and DV-201..203 for routine use; those documents stay as the detailed spec of what each area needs. The Phase 0 Round 2 items (stock-camera comparison, DNG in a RAW viewer) are still separate, one-time jobs and can wait until you are ready.
